Porottoz can calculate how much you can spend on any given day and will recalculate this as time passes so that, if you don't spend today, you can spend more tomorrow!

Basically, we take all the money you have to spend and divide it over the number of days until your next payday, so you know how much you can spend.

To create a budget

Just click/touch the ➕ button at the top of the page. This will open a page where you can provide:

  • An optional title
  • The final date of your budget, which might be your next payday
  • Your current balance, or how much money to include in this budget
  • And, optionally, how much you might want to exclude from spending in case you need to use it on bills

To update a budget

Click/touch the ✏️ button. This will open the same page as when you created a budget and you will be able to make any necessary adjustments.

To delete a budget

Click/touch the 🗑️ button. You will be presented with a message to confirm your choice.

When you make a purchase

And have to update the current balance, click/touch the 💸 button and enter the purchase amount. Porottoz will adjust the balance accordingly. Use the 💰 button when you receive money.
