# JOPR
The war against recruitment agencies and HR departments is merciless. Don't become another discarded victim. Instead, take the fight to the enemy! Your weapon of choice will be devastatingly targetted CVs and cover letters that will take no prisoners. A custom made CV for EVERY job application! Yes, you heard right! Each CV will specifically address all the requirements of a particular job. Each CV will be worded in the same language used in the original job description. Each CV will highlight all the tech and previous jobs that relate to the application in question. Their armies of middle managers will be no match for your tactical CV nukes!

## Features
- No signup required. All your data is stored on your device and you don't need to provide anyone your personal details.
- Free. 
- Store your career history in one place. Maintain your personal details, skills, education, previous roles, and interests.
- Generate individual CVs and cover letters for every job you apply for.
- Track the results of your job applications, attach relevant files, keep track of relevant contacts and your interactions with them.
- Watch IT trends based on actual job postings.
