# JOPR
The war against recruitment agencies and HR departments is merciless. Don't become another discarded victim. Instead, take the fight to the enemy! Your weapon of choice will be devastatingly targetted CVs and cover letters that will take no prisoners. A custom made CV for EVERY job application! Yes, you heard right! Each CV will specifically address all the requirements of a particular job. Each CV will be worded in the same language used in the original job description. Each CV will highlight all the tech and previous jobs that relate to the application in question. Their armies of middle managers will be no match for your tactical CV nukes!

## Key Features
- No signup required. All your data is stored locally on your device and you don't need to provide anyone of your personal details to an external service.
- Free. 
- Store your career history in one place, along with your personal details, skills, education, previous roles, and interests.
- Generate individual CVs and cover letters for every job you apply for.
- Track the results of your job applications, attach relevant files, keep track of relevant contacts and your interactions with them.
- Watch IT trends based on actual job postings.

## Detailed Feature List
- Upload & Download whole application state and data to make it easy to backup data and transfer amongst devices

### Job Tracker
- create job through ai reading pasted text
- create job manually by entering role title, type, company, duration, source, renumartion, status, link, location, description, agency, contact
- lists all jobs by date and status to easily focus on open roles
- can expand individual roles for quick details like type, renumeration, info link, and attachment links
- update status and add notes
- download job details in json format in case youd like to use it in an external ai request
- attach files like CVs and cover letters used in the application
- generate job specific CV
- generate job specific cover letter
- view all job details and attachments
- edit job details
- delete a job

### Company List
- list all companies and recruitment agencies
- add company details like name, phone, industry, address, url, email
- view all company details
- edit company details
- delete a company

### Contacts List
- add contact details like name, phone, email, address, url, description
- view all contact details
- edit contact details
- delete a contact

### Profile & Work History
- download profile and work history in json format for use in external ai requests
- upload a CV or resume so the ai can extract the relevant profile and work history data
- edit profile data like name, phone, website residency status, email, adress, seek url, linkedin url, personal summary, skills, interests
- view, add, edit, delete education details like title, institution, year
- view, add, edit, delete job details like company, title, work type, location, start time, end time, tech used, responsibilities, projects
