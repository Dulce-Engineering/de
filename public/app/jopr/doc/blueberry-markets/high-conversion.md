### 2. Performance & Latency
* **Eliminate Hero Layout Shifts (CLS)**
* **Aggressive Critical-Path Asset Inlining**
* **Defer Non-Critical Runtimes**
* **Native Over Heavy Frameworks**

### 3. Progressive Information Architecture
| Page Section | Core Purpose | Conversion Lever |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Proof Bar** | Defuses initial skepticism | Recognizable logos, user ratings, GitHub stars, press mentions |
| **Problem vs. Solution** | Validates pain points | Clear "Before vs. After" comparison or feature highlights |
| **Feature Deep Dive** | Demonstrates capability | Focused cards with tangible benefits, not abstract feature lists |
| **Objection Busting (FAQ)** | Removes purchase/signup anxiety | Plain-English answers on pricing, data privacy, cancellation, exports |
| **Sticky / Final CTA** | Captures scrolled intent | Repeat the primary action at the bottom of the long scroll |

### 4. Remove Conversion Roadblocks & Friction
* **Kill the Navigation Maze**
* **Single-Field or Frictionless Onboarding** Request only the email address upfront, offer an interactive "zero-login" demo/sandbox.
* **Explicit Privacy & Security Badges** State your privacy posture clearly right below the CTA (e.g., "No credit card required • Zero tracking • Local-first storage").
