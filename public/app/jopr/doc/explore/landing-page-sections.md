Based on the *WarGames* aesthetic and the functional goals of a modern PWA landing page, the page should be structured into **6 distinct, chronological sections**.

The order is strategically designed to first hook the user with the theme, establish immediate trust via your data privacy model, detail the features, and then drive them straight into the app.

---

### 1. Section 1: The Command Console (Hero Section)

* **What it contains:** A striking, green-on-black terminal style interface. A high-impact, theme-heavy headline, a concise subheadline explaining that JOPR is an AI-powered, local-first job tracker/CV builder, and a single, dominant Call-to-Action (CTA) button to launch the PWA.
* **Purpose:** To capture immediate attention and instantly filter for your target audience (tech-savvy job seekers who love a good retro aesthetic).

### 2. Section 2: Tactical Objectives (The Core Pillars)

* **What it contains:** A high-level layout showing the 3 to 4 main value propositions of the app using military/Cold War terminology: **Air-Gapped Privacy** (Local-first), **Target Intelligence** (AI parsing), **Payload Generation** (Custom CVs/Cover letters), and **Theater Mapping** (Tracking & Trends).
* **Purpose:** To give skimming users an instant understanding of what the app does mechanically before they commit to reading further.

### 3. Section 3: System Capabilities (Detailed Feature Manifest)

* **What it contains:** A clean, organized grid or interactive dashboard layout detailing the granular features you listed. It will be broken down into sub-modules:
* *Strike Operations* (The Job Tracker, AI text-pasting, status updates, JSON exports).
* *Dossier Management* (Profile, work history, resume ingestion).
* *Network Intercepts* (Company and Contact rosters).


* **Purpose:** To prove that JOPR is a robust, full-featured professional tool, not just a thematic novelty site.

### 4. Section 4: Security Briefing (The Air-Gap Guarantee)

* **What it contains:** A visually distinct, highlighted blockquote styled like a "Classified" or "Top Secret" military brief. This explicitly breaks down how the local storage works, the complete absence of a signup sheet or cloud database, and how users can back up/transfer their own JSON data state.
* **Purpose:** To handle the user’s single biggest objection—*privacy concerns regarding their personal career data and AI tools*—and turn it into your ultimate selling point.

### 5. Section 5: Global Deployment (How It Works / The Workflow)

* **What it contains:** A step-by-step visual or textual progression of a typical user flow:
1. *Ingest Legacy Dossier* (Upload resume to populate profile).
2. *Intercept Target* (Paste a new job description).
3. *Deploy Payload* (Generate the tailored CV/Cover letter).
4. *Monitor Theater* (Track status and watch IT trends).


* **Purpose:** To lower the cognitive friction of using a new application by showing exactly how easy, fast, and tactical the daily routine is.

### 6. Section 6: Launch Protocol (Footer & Final CTA)

* **What it contains:** A stark, minimalist closing section with a final, large button to launch the app, coupled with a thematic Easter egg quote (e.g., *"A strange game. The only winning move is to apply. Shall we begin?"*), alongside copyright details and a link to your project repository if applicable.
* **Purpose:** To catch users who scrolled to the bottom to find a way in, ensuring they don't have to scroll all the way back up to launch the app.
