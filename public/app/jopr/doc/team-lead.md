
### 1. Technical Direction & Engineering Standards

* Rreview high-level designs (RFCs / ADRs)
* Align tasks with platform standards
* Establishing definition-of-done (DoD)
* set code review norms
* set test coverage standards (unit, integration, end-to-end)
* manage refactoring
* manage dependencies
* establish telemetry (logging, metrics, alerting)
* establishing incident response runbooks, and conducting blameless post-mortems.

### 2. Delivery & Process Management

* **Sprint Planning & Backlog Refinement:** Breaking down epics into actionable user stories, estimating effort, identifying technical risks, and sequencing dependencies.
* **Unblocking & Bottleneck Removal:** Acting as the first line of escalation when engineers encounter blocking dependencies, environmental issues, or cross-team friction.
* **Capacity Planning:** Calculating team velocity, assessing PTO/on-call loads, and setting realistic commitments with product managers.
* **Continuous Process Improvement:** Facilitating retrospectives to turn team pain points into concrete action items that optimize the development cycle (CI/CD pipelines, lead time to deploy).

### 3. People Leadership & Team Growth

* **Mentorship & Coaching:** Conducting regular 1-on-1s focused on career progression, identifying skill gaps, and delegating stretch projects.
* **Onboarding & Knowledge Sharing:** Building team documentation, onboarding pathways, and fostering practices like pair programming or brown-bag sessions.
* **Conflict Resolution & Psychological Safety:** Mediating technical debates constructively, balancing strong opinions, and maintaining a high-trust, blame-free environment.
* **Hiring & Evaluation:** Participating in interview loops, designing practical technical assessments, and contributing to performance reviews.

### 4. Stakeholder & Cross-Functional Alignment

* **Translating Business to Tech (and vice versa):** Communicating complex architectural trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders (Product, Design, Commercial) in terms of risk, cost, and timelines.
* **Shielding the Team:** Filtering incoming organizational noise, ad-hoc requests, and scope creep to preserve deep work and focus.
* **Cross-Team Coordination:** Aligning contracts, APIs, and release schedules with upstream/downstream engineering teams.

### Interview Tip: The "70/30 Split" Narrative

When asked about your day-to-day balance, frame your role as navigating a split:

> *"My core responsibility is force multiplication. While I keep my technical edge sharp by tackling proof-of-concepts, reviewing critical architecture, and unblocking hard bugs, roughly 70% of my value comes from orchestrating delivery, coaching engineers, and ensuring product and engineering stay in lockstep."*
