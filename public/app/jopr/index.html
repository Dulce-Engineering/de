GLOBAL RECRUITMENT MANEUVERS: THE ONLY WINNING MOVE IS TO STRATEGICALLY OUT-GUN THEM.

JOPR is a free, local-first tactical job tracker and AI-powered CV generator designed to turn your career history into devastatingly targeted application nukes. No signups. No cloud databases. Just raw tactical advantage.

The enemy? Armies of middle managers, faceless HR departments, and black-box ATS algorithms. If you send them a generic resume, you are marching your career into an ambush. To survive, you need a custom-tailored counter-strike for *every single application*.

JOPR gives you the command center to orchestrate a hyper-targeted, high-frequency deployment of flawless resumes and cover letters, matching the enemy's language exactly.

Air-Gapped Privacy

No signups. No external tracking. All your profile data, work history, and target intelligence stay locked down locally on your device's browser.

Target Acquisition (AI)

Feed the terminal a raw text paste of any job description. JOPR's internal intelligence matrix automatically extracts the role, company, salary, and requirements instantly.

Payload Customization

Generate localized, job-specific CVs and cover letters that mirror the exact terminology, tech stack, and keywords of the target posting.

Theater Mapping

Track your active campaigns, map key enemy contacts (recruiters, hiring managers), and monitor emerging IT trends based on real-world battlefield data.

Threat Intelligence (Profile & History) * **Legacy Ingestion:** Drop your existing resume straight into the terminal; JOPR parses and structures your history automatically. * **Complete Arsenal:** Manage your deep dossier of skills, projects, responsibilities, and specific tech stacks in one master command post. * **Data Sovereignty:** Export your entire system state or specific profiles as raw JSON strings at any moment to keep under your own lock and key. Strike Operations (Job Tracker) * **Campaign Ledger:** A centralized, chronological triage screen to filter open, pending, and secured roles. * **Dossier Management:** Attach specific CV versions, cover letters, and logs of every communication/interaction with the target organization. * **External AI Interop:** Don't want to use built-in AI? Instantly download specific job parameters as formatted JSON to pass to your own external LLM terminal. Network Intercepts (Companies & Contacts) * **Asset Tracking:** Maintain a deep rolodex of recruitment agencies, corporate targets, and individual field contacts (phone, email, secure URLs).
CLASSIFIED: SECURITY BRIEFING

JOPR operates entirely client-side. We do not want your personal details, your work history, or your contact list. Your browser is your bunker. Back up your data by downloading your encrypted state file, and transfer it between devices manually. Total data sovereignty.
