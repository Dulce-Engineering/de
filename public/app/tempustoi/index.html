TempusToi is a simple task scheduling tool that helps you stay on top of deadlines.

It displays a sorted list of countdown timers, visually representing the time remaining for each task at a glance.

Unlike cloud-based planners, TempusToi requires no online account or subscription—it runs entirely on your PC or mobile device, keeping your tasks private and accessible anytime.

Please note that, if this app is not installed, due to browser restrictions sound notifications need to be manually activated every time on startup.
