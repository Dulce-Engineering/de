Two approaches that would give an immediate, glanceable sense of urgency without breaking the mechanical watch / instrument aesthetic:

### 1. Urgency Tint & Dimming (Visual Hierarchy)

Right now, an event 12 hours away looks visually identical in weight and color to an event 11 days away because all four segmented rings are rendered with the same bright gold ticks and dark backgrounds.

* **De-emphasize zeroed / inactive units:** If an event has `0 days`, dim that ring (or drop its tick opacity to 20%). When an event enters the final hour, dim both `days` and `hours`. The eye will instantly recognize that cards with fewer bright dials are imminently arriving.
* **Warmth shift based on proximity:**
* **> 7 days:** Cool / muted forest green ticks with lower contrast.
* **24 to 48 hours:** Standard warm amber gold.
* **< 24 hours:** High-contrast golden cream with a subtle amber border glow or illuminated title pip.
* **< 2 hours:** Pulsing second ring or ticking needle accent.

---

### 2. A "Proximity Horizon" Gauge (Single Relative Complication)

Because time-to-event is split across four distinct modular units (Days, Hours, Mins, Secs), the brain has to read the numbers rather than parse a shape. Adding a single relative meter makes proximity intuitive at a 50-millisecond glance.

* **Logarithmic / Tiered Horizon Bar:** Place a slim 3-segment or 4-segment horizontal bead track directly beneath the event title:
`[ >30d ] ── [ 7d ] ── [ 24h ] ── [ NOW ]`
A glowing pip or sliding mechanical indicator moves along this rail.
* **Master Proximity Ring (Chronograph Style):** Replace or prefix the first ring with an overall relative arc:
* For events beyond 24h, the outer ring represents days remaining out of a fixed window (e.g., 30 or 14 days).
* Once within 24 hours, the gauge "shifts gear" into a countdown circle that drains like an egg timer or nautical chronometer bezel, showing how close to zero it is as a single sweeping wedge.

---

### 3. Progressive Card Scale or Left-Edge "Fuse"

* **The Edge Fuse:** Add a colored vertical strip along the left edge of each event card. For distant events, it is thin and deep green. As the event nears:
* Under 3 days: Turns solid gold.
* Under 24 hours: Expands to 4px and ticks/glows.

* **Top-Card Elevation:** Give events happening within 24 hours a slightly lighter card backing or a subtle brass card bezel, letting them visually pop out of the dark green background compared to events scheduled weeks out.
