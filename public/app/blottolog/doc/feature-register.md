1. Core Tracking & Logging (The Essentials)
The biggest hurdle for users is the effort it takes to log a drink, especially as the night goes on. This needs to be as fast as possible.

Quick Add Favorites: One-tap buttons for the user's most frequent drinks (e.g., "My usual IPA", "House Red").
Standard Drink Database: A searchable library of common beers, wines, and spirits with pre-filled ABV (Alcohol by Volume).
Custom Drink Builder: Sliders to input custom volumes (oz/ml) and custom ABV percentages for mixed drinks.
Barcode Scanner: Quick scanning for store-bought cans and bottles.
Retroactive Logging: Ability to easily add a drink they forgot to log earlier, adjusting the timestamp.

2. Biological Metrics & Safety (The Value Add)
Personalized Profile: Input for weight, height, age, and biological sex to accurately calculate metabolism rates.
Real-Time BAC Estimator: A living chart showing their current estimated Blood Alcohol Concentration.
"Time to Sober" Countdown: A timer predicting when their BAC will return to 0.00%.
Custom Limits & Alerts: Push notifications when approaching a user-set threshold (e.g., "You're approaching your 3-drink limit for tonight!").
Ride-Share Integration: A quick-launch button to open Uber, Lyft, or local taxi services if their BAC is over the legal driving limit.

3. Analytics & Insights (Retention)
The "Wallet" Tracker: Estimating money spent on alcohol based on average prices or user input.
Calorie Counter: Showing the hidden caloric impact of their drinks (a major motivator for many to cut back).
Historical Trends: Weekly, monthly, and yearly charts showing consumption patterns (e.g., "You drink 40% of your weekly total on Thursdays").
Health Integrations: Syncing with Apple Health or Google Fit to correlate drinking habits with sleep quality or resting heart rate.

4. Wellness & Interventions
Hydration Prompts: Reminders to drink a glass of water after logging an alcoholic beverage.
"Dry Days" Streak Tracker: Gamifying days where the user consumes zero alcohol.
Journaling/Notes: A small text box to note why they drank or how they felt the next morning, helping identify triggers.
