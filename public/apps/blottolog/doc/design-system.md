# Design System

## Page
- Light gradient background resembling a map parchment

## Primary Button
- Large, pill-shaped or slightly rounded rectangular button, styled to look like brass or polished wood.
- Bright gold/brass metallic gradient background (ties directly into the exact styling of the gold Doubloon Action Button).
- Bold, high-contrast dark text (e.g., `Ink Brown`).
- Heavy bottom shadow (`box-shadow`) to give it a physical, tactile weight, like pressing down on a coin or a wooden lever.

## Secondary Button
- Same physical shape and heavy tactical feel as the Primary Button, but visually subdued so it doesn't fight the Doubloon for attention.
- Solid dark background (`Nautical Blue` or `Ink Brown`).
- Contrasting gold or tan text (`Parchment Tan`) with a matching thin border.
- Hover/Active states invert colors or sink the shadow.

- Treasure map background and ship position indicator
- Strictly formatted next-drink countdown text and clock

## Drinks List
- Dynamic flex-wrap parent container
- Individual text-less image components representing drinks like a mug or bottle
- Displays finished drinks and unfinished drinks separately
